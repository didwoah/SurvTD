## Idea review — SurvTD: Duration-Discounted Temporal-Difference Consistency for Dynamic Survival Analysis under Irregular Observation

**Decomposition**

- **Problem / gap:** Temporal-consistency survival methods (TCSR, DeepTCSR) relate consecutive predictions through a transition that "assume[s] an exogenous uniform unit step Delta t = 1", so on irregularly observed trajectories the inter-observation relation is either undefined or wrong; and the backward update is claimed to "renormalise[] the next-step lifetime distribution by dividing by the interval survival probability, which diverges exactly in the high-risk regime where S approaches zero".
- **Method (the move):** Keep the consistency objective; swap the inter-observation operator. Carry the target network's remaining-lifetime distribution backwards by a rightward shift of the observed elapsed duration (the renewal identity `R_j = R_{j+1} + Delta t_j`) composed with C51's triangular categorical projection onto a fixed bin grid; attach a duration discount `gamma_j = S_theta-(Delta t_j)`; mix multi-step targets with `lambda_j = lambda^(Delta t_j / delta_s)`; fit by Cramér distance; complete censored terminal tails with IPCW from a Kaplan–Meier censoring estimate.
- **Why it should work:** The substitution is defended "by the properties it preserves rather than by a performance claim" — `Pi Phi` "conserves unit probability mass exactly, is non-expansive in the Cramer metric, and yields an affine target map whose modulus under a frozen target network is bounded by the interval discount". Hence a valid distributional Bellman target "at every interval length" rather than only at `Delta t = 1`.
- **Assumptions inferred (not stated in the candidate):**
  1. Observation times are non-informative — the elapsed duration and the fact of being observed at `t_{j+1}` carry no information about latent severity beyond what the encoder already sees. Nothing in Steps 1–8 conditions on the observation process.
  2. The event indicator `I[event in the interval]` is observed for every interval, which is what makes Step 5 well defined — and which is also what makes `gamma_j` redundant in Step 5 (see attacks 2.2 / 4.2).
  3. Censoring is independent of covariates, since Step 6 uses "a Kaplan-Meier estimate of the censoring distribution" (marginal), not a conditional censoring model.
  4. The bin grid `K, delta_s` is fine enough that the triangular projection's smoothing bias is second-order relative to the effect being measured. Step 2 fixes the grid "from the cohort follow-up quantile" but the candidate never bounds the projection bias.
  5. The characterisation of TCSR/DeepTCSR's update as a *division* is accurate. I could not verify this from the packet; the run's own `phase0/lit_table.md` records the same one-line claim ("`÷ S(Δt)` division diverges under high risk"), which is the candidate's own upstream summary, not independent corroboration.

**Naive-baseline audit:** branch **2 — incremental**, with a branch-3 mitigation, and I am deliberately **not** firing the hard floor. My independently constructed naive version, written without reference to the candidate's stated baseline: *"Take TCSR's renewal consistency. The next-visit lifetime distribution is the current one shifted left by the elapsed time, so the backward target is the next-visit distribution shifted right by `Delta t`. Since `Delta t` is not a multiple of the bin width, linearly interpolate the shifted mass onto the grid — i.e. C51's projection. Train with the same consistency loss."* That naive version is not an *alternative* that makes SurvTD superfluous; it **is** SurvTD, minus `gamma_j`, minus the duration-geometric `lambda`, minus the IPCW tail. The hard floor is calibrated for the case where a cheaper construction achieves the goal and the proposed machinery is therefore unnecessary; here the naive construction and the proposal coincide, which is a **size** finding, not a validity finding. The branch-3 mitigation is real and named in the packet: the two parent literatures "returned ... as two separate literatures; the candidate sits at their intersection ... unoccupied", so the obvious-in-hindsight move has in fact not been made. The correct response is to strip the decoration and defend the minimal move honestly — not to abandon it, and not to keep presenting it as an operator-theoretic contribution.

**Novel-but-empty detector:** passes. I can state what the idea predicts (discrimination and calibration on irregular cohorts move toward the unit-step baseline when duration scaling is removed), what it changes (the inter-observation target operator), and what would count against it (NC-B leaving the gain intact, which would attribute it to regularisation).

**Strongest point:** The substitution is argued from properties preserved rather than performance promised — mass conservation and Cramér non-expansiveness — and the theory claim is voluntarily scoped down to "per-iteration contraction under a frozen target, not convergence of the joint two-timescale scheme"; that discipline is rare at idea stage and is what makes the proposal reviewable at all rather than merely plausible.

**Most fixable weakness:** Redeclare the load-bearing variable as `Delta t_j` inside `Phi_{+Delta t_j}` and rebuild NC-A around it (unit shift `Phi_{+delta_s}` versus duration shift `Phi_{+Delta t_j}`, encoder and `Delta t` features held fixed) — or write `gamma_j` explicitly into the Step 5 target — since as stated the primary negative control intervenes on a quantity that does not appear in the update it is meant to ablate, which converts the idea's best asset (its falsification design) into its most exposed one.

## Two-layer verdict

**Hard floor — checked, not fired.**

- *Prior work matching on all four scoop axes:* no. The scoop report gives "Overlap level: 2 / 5 — adjacent lineage, distinct technical move", with TCSR matching on framing and partial insight only. Floor does not fire. I note for the panel that the report itself flags "OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, DBLP and Crossref were all unreachable this run", so the no-scoop finding is provisional on two channels — provisional is not a floor trigger, but it is a reason not to treat non-duplication as an asset.
- *Naive-baseline audit returns "naive suffices":* branch 2 fired, floor deliberately **not**. Reasoning stated in full in the audit above: the naive construction coincides with the proposal rather than superseding it, which is a §6.1 size finding. Firing `abandon` here would abandon every honest minimal-method paper, which is not what the floor is for. I record this as a judgment call so a synthesising context can see exactly where I drew the line.
- *Anti-pattern composition whose mitigation cannot be inserted:* no. The `composition_note`'s claim that the two patterns "share the inter-observation transition operator as their intermediate object" is coherent, and every mitigation I would demand (rewire `gamma_j`, rebuild NC-A, add the encoder-matched arm) is insertable within the stated 18-GPU-hour budget.
- *D collapses:* no. D = 4; falsification is constructible and largely already constructed.

**Soft judgment: `revise`.**

This is not a trivial borderline, so `advance` is not available. It is a correct, feasible, honestly-scoped operator generalisation whose write-up prices itself above its size and whose primary negative control intervenes on the wrong variable. Both are fixable on paper, before a single GPU-hour is spent. My honest area-chair read, stated plainly because it is the verdict this seat exists to give: **as currently framed this is a good workshop-to-mid-tier paper wearing a NeurIPS operator-theory jacket.** The version that is genuinely NeurIPS-scale is the one that stops defending `gamma_j` and instead attacks informative observation — the part of "irregular observation" that is actually open — using the same shift-and-project machinery with an intensity-aware discount. That reframing is available and is a strictly larger idea than the one on the table.

**revision_targets[]**

1. **Wire the load-bearing variable to the mechanism.** Either write `gamma_j` explicitly into the Step 5 target and say what it multiplies, or drop it and redeclare `Delta t_j` inside `Phi_{+Delta t_j}` as `load_bearing_variable`. As written, Steps 1–8 are unchanged by deleting `gamma_j`.
2. **Rebuild NC-A around the redeclared variable.** The primary control should ablate the duration shift (`Phi_{+delta_s}` vs `Phi_{+Delta t_j}`), encoder and `Delta t` features held fixed — not a discount that does not appear in the update.
3. **Confront or explicitly scope out informative observation times.** Add `Lin2001` / `Alaa2017` to `differentiation_from_lit` with a real delta, and either extend the discount to be intensity-aware or narrow the title and motivation to *irregular but non-informative* observation. The current breadth is not matched by the mechanism.
4. **Verify the "division" characterisation of TCSR/DeepTCSR against the actual update.** If their backward recursion is a multiplication by the interval survival rather than a division by it, the divergence motivation is a strawman and the whole `assumption_audit_and_pivot` framing must be restated around off-grid shifting instead.
5. **Price the contribution at its true size.** Lead the abstract with the machinery-free sentence, demote the duration-geometric `lambda` and the IPCW tail to secondary contributions, and cut the signature-term inflation. A minimal move defended honestly reviews better than a small move presented as operator theory.
6. **Add the encoder-matched arm.** Same continuous-time encoder, terminal likelihood versus SurvTD consistency loss, to answer §1.3 — otherwise a reviewer will read the gains as encoder gains.
7. **Repair the numeric provenance and the boundary regimes.** Source the seed-to-seed SD behind the `0.03` bar or strike it; state what happens to the contraction bound as `Delta t -> 0` and `gamma_j -> 1`; replace the marginal Kaplan–Meier censoring estimate with a conditional one for MIMIC-IV.
