## Idea review — SurvTD: Duration-Discounted Temporal-Difference Consistency for Dynamic Survival Analysis under Irregular Observation

**Decomposition**

- **Problem / gap:** Temporal-consistency survival methods (TCSR, DeepTCSR) enforce agreement between predictions at consecutive observations under an assumed uniform unit step, so "a whole interval passed without an event" carries one bit regardless of whether the interval was six minutes or six months; and the backward update is written as a conditioning step that divides by the interval survival probability, which is undefined at non-integer Δt and numerically explodes as `S → 0`.
- **Method (the move):** Keep the consistency objective; replace the update operator. The next-observation lifetime pmf from an EMA target network is carried backward by a rightward shift of Δt on the remaining-lifetime axis and projected back onto the fixed bin grid with a triangular (linear-interpolation) kernel, with overflow accumulated in a terminal absorbing bin. An "interval duration discount" `γ_j = S_θ⁻(Δt_j)` is the model's own predicted probability of surviving the interval. Multi-step targets use `λ_j = λ^(Δt_j/δ_s)`. Loss is Cramér distance on CDFs, semi-gradient through θ only. Censored terminal observations get an IPCW tail completion.
- **Why it should work:** `ΠΦ` conserves mass exactly and is non-expansive in the Cramér metric, so the target is a valid distribution at any real Δt; under a frozen target the map is affine with modulus bounded by `γ_j`; duration-geometric λ makes the effective bootstrapping horizon invariant to sampling rate.
- **Assumptions inferred (not stated in the candidate):** (i) observation times are non-informative — Δt is exogenous to latent risk; (ii) the remaining-lifetime support is well covered by K bins so that terminal-bin overflow is rare; (iii) the KM censoring estimator used for IPCW is marginal and correctly specified; (iv) `γ_j` enters the loss somewhere — it does not appear in the Step-5 target expression as written.

**Naive-baseline audit: branch 1 (application-grade).**

Constructed independently, not from the author's foil. What a competent person in this subfield builds first:

- **Naive A** — a discrete-time hazard head over remaining-lifetime bins (Dynamic-DeepHit / DDH-style), on a time-aware encoder (GRU-D, time-LSTM, neural CDE) that takes Δt as an input, trained on the standard right-censored discrete likelihood evaluated at *every* visit (landmarking at each observation). This handles irregular observation completely and emits a valid distribution at every visit by construction, with no consistency term at all. Naive A does **not** suffice for the candidate's goal, because the value of a consistency objective over per-visit likelihood is the TCSR claim and is empirically supported upstream.
- **Naive B** — Naive A plus a consistency regulariser written in the *backward* direction: match `p_j` to the target-network's `p_{j+1}` shifted by Δt, filling the in-interval portion from the model, with linear interpolation because Δt is not a bin multiple.

Naive B is SurvTD minus the vocabulary. The diff that remains is: Cramér instead of KL, the EMA target network (explicitly inherited from DeepTCSR and acknowledged), the duration-geometric λ, and the IPCW tail completion.

This is branch 1 rather than branch 2 for one reason: the premise "the backward update must condition on interval survival, hence divide" is genuinely false, and noticing that is the contribution. But branch 1's rider applies with full force — the confrontation *is* a textbook tool from a neighbouring field (linear interpolation of a shifted pmf onto a fixed grid is C51's projection), so the paper must name the domain-specific structure that made this instance unsolved or call the contribution application-grade honestly. The one structure that qualifies is named: right-censoring forces a tail completion under a projected distributional target, which has no analogue in reward distributions. That is a real but modest piece. The honest label is **application-grade with one survival-specific component**, and the write-up currently claims more than that.

The audit slides to branch 2 if the `÷ S` characterisation of the ancestor is a misreading — see attack 5.3.

**Strongest point:** the negative-control design is above idea-stage norm — a placebo arm that keeps Δt in the encoder, a regular-sampling cohort where the intervention is a no-op by construction, and a duration-scrambling control — and Step 5 volunteers the contraction claim's exact limit ("not convergence of the joint two-timescale scheme") instead of overclaiming it.

**Most fixable weakness:** `γ_j` never appears in the stated target, so the primary negative control ablates a quantity the algorithm does not use; writing Step 5 as the explicit `(1−γ_j)·(in-interval density) + γ_j·(ΠΦ p_θ⁻)` mixture — or relocating the load-bearing variable to the operator — would raise B-soundness and D together.

## Two-layer verdict

**Hard floor — checked, not fired.**
- Prior work matching on all four scoop axes: no. Scoop-radar reports "Overlap level: 2 / 5 — adjacent lineage, distinct technical move" and no four-axis match. (Noted: that finding is provisional — "OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, DBLP and Crossref were all unreachable this run." The distributional-RL-over-temporal-abstraction and GVF/transition-dependent-discount literatures are exactly what a two-channel arXiv/OpenReview sweep under-recalls, and those are the two places I would expect a near-neighbour to surface. Re-run before committing the budget.)
- Naive-baseline audit returns *naive suffices*: **no** — branch 1, though only just, and the branch-1 rider forces an application-grade framing.
- Anti-pattern composition with an uninsertable mitigation: no. `composition_note` names the shared intermediate object (the inter-observation transition operator) and the composition is coherent.
- D collapses: no. D = 4; falsification is constructible and partly well designed.

**Soft judgment: `revise`.**
The idea is on target for the gap it names and is cheap to run, but the delta as written is substantially lexical, the declared load-bearing variable does not appear in the declared mechanism, and the cheapest competing fixes — a finer grid, and the division-free multiplicative form of the ancestor's own identity — are neither argued against nor included as arms. None of that is fatal; all of it is answerable, and most of it before a single GPU-hour is spent.

**revision_targets[]**

1. **Put `γ_j` in the target, or change the load-bearing variable.** Write Step 5 as the explicit mixture `T p_j = (1 − γ_j)·(in-interval event density) + γ_j·(ΠΦ_{+Δt_j} p_θ⁻)` and say how it relates to the empirical-indicator form, or move `load_bearing_variable` to the operator `ΠΦ`. As written, NC-A intervenes on a quantity that does not appear in the computation.
2. **Add the grid-refinement baseline.** DeepTCSR on a bin grid fine enough that `Δt/δ_s` is near-integer, multi-step, Δt in the encoder. This is the cheapest thing that makes the problem go away and it is currently absent from the design.
3. **Add an ablation that isolates `ΠΦ` from `÷ S`.** Same encoder, λ, γ, and grid; swap only the operator, with `S` clamped or log-parameterised in the `÷ S` arm so the comparison is against the fix a competent implementer would already have applied — not against an unguarded division.
4. **State the non-informative-observation precondition, or handle it.** Δt is risk-dependent in ICU and clinic cohorts; either declare the assumption explicitly and confine the claim, or add inverse-intensity weighting on the observation process (Lin2001 / Alaa2017 are already in the retrieved literature as exactly this residue).
5. **Restate the positioning honestly against distributional RL.** Retire "the shift is a renewal identity rather than a reward translation" — it is a reward translation. Concede that the projection and its Cramér non-expansiveness are imported, and rest the delta on censored tail completion under a projected distributional target, which genuinely has no analogue upstream.
6. **Position the endogenous discount against transition-dependent discounting.** A state-dependent γ that equals a continuation probability is the standard termination-function construction in the GVF/Horde line; the delta is that the model predicts its own γ, and that must be argued, not asserted.
7. **Show the degenerate fixed point is excluded.** All mass in the terminal absorbing bin satisfies the consistency recursion exactly; give the argument (or the arm) showing the event and IPCW terms dominate under the censoring rates of the target cohorts.
8. **Fix the 0.03 bar's provenance.** Report the actual seed-to-seed SD with its source rather than deriving the SD backwards from the chosen gap.
