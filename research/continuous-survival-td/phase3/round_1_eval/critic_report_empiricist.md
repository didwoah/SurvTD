## Idea review — SurvTD: Duration-Discounted Temporal-Difference Consistency for Dynamic Survival Analysis under Irregular Observation

**Decomposition**

- **Problem / gap:** Temporal-consistency survival methods (TCSR, DeepTCSR) bootstrap between consecutive observations under an assumed uniform unit step — "assume an exogenous uniform unit step Delta t = 1, so the non-event signal carried by an interval is one bit regardless of how much time elapsed" — and obtain their backward target by dividing by the interval survival probability, which "diverges exactly in the high-risk regime where S approaches zero."
- **Method (the move):** Two coupled substitutions in the Bellman target. (i) The interval discount becomes duration-dependent and endogenous: "gamma_j = S_theta-(Delta t_j) computed from an EMA target network." (ii) The division is replaced by a transport operator: "carried backwards by a rightward shift Phi_{+Delta t_j} (the renewal identity R_j = R_{j+1} + Delta t_j) composed with a categorical projection Pi onto the fixed bin grid, instead of by division." Plus a duration-geometric λ-return ("lambda_j = lambda^(Delta t_j / delta_s)"), an IPCW tail completion at censoring, and a Cramér-distance fit.
- **Why it should work:** Defended by preserved properties rather than by performance: "Pi Phi is non-expansive in the Cramer metric and conserves total mass exactly," and "with theta- held frozen the map is affine in p and its Cramer-metric modulus is bounded by gamma_j."
- **Assumptions inferred (not stated in the candidate):** that the named cohorts are irregularly sampled to a degree that makes S(Δt) differ materially from S(δ_s); that observation times are non-informative, or that informativeness is not the source of any gain; that censoring is independent of covariates (required by "inverse probability of censoring from a Kaplan-Meier estimate of the censoring distribution"); that baselines are run on a shared encoder so a win is attributable to the loss; that the "four methods" include TCSR and DeepTCSR and that both are reproducible.

**Overall: 58/100 · Verdict: borderline**
No gate fired (A = 3, C = 4, D = 3).

**Naive-baseline audit:** branch (1), *conditionally* — and the condition is the design's central omission. My independently constructed naive version is not the candidate's strawman: take DeepTCSR unchanged, append Δt as an encoder input, and evaluate its existing renormalisation at the true elapsed duration with the denominator clamped at S ≥ ε. That is what any competent practitioner builds first, in an afternoon. The candidate's premise — that division is *unusable* at real interval lengths — is confronted theoretically (mass conservation, non-expansiveness) and is genuinely load-bearing if true, which is what makes this branch 1 rather than branch 2. But clamped division is numerically usable, and mass can be restored by renormalising the projected pmf, so whether the premise is actually false is an empirical question. **That naive arm is absent from the proposed comparison.** As specified, the experiment cannot distinguish branch 1 from branch 2. This does not fire the hard floor — I have no results and must not invent them — but it is the single most consequential hole in the instrument.

**Novel-but-empty detector:** passes. The idea states what it predicts (discrimination drops toward the unit-step baseline under NC-A on irregular cohorts, unchanged on regular ones), what would count against it, and a numeric bar. It is falsifiable in principle; my objections are to the calibration of the instrument, not to its existence.

**Strongest point:** NC-A is a properly constructed placebo — it holds the encoder, the timestamp inputs, the λ schedule and every hyperparameter fixed and ablates only the operator's use of duration, which is the correct way to separate "the model sees Δt" from "the operator scales with Δt"; combined with Step 2's declaration that "K and delta_s are set from the cohort follow-up quantile and held fixed across all methods in a comparison" and an equal "fifty-trial hyperparameter search per method," this is a more disciplined parity story than most idea cards carry.

**Most fixable weakness:** The design has no dose-response arm — the simulated tumour-growth ODE and Ornstein-Uhlenbeck cohorts let irregularity be dialled continuously, and showing the gain over the unit-step baseline grow monotonically with sampling-interval dispersion (and vanish at zero dispersion) would isolate the claimed cause far more convincingly than any number of head-to-head wins on four fixed cohorts; it costs a sweep on data that is already free to generate.

## Two-layer verdict

**Hard floor — checked, none fired.**
- No prior work matches on all four scoop axes: the scoop report states "Overlap level: 2 / 5 — adjacent lineage, distinct technical move" and "No contemporaneous scoop found." (Its own caveat that OpenAlex/S2/DBLP/Crossref were unreachable is noted, but a provisional no-scoop is not a floor trigger.)
- The naive-baseline audit returns branch 1, not *naive suffices*. I cannot assert that the clamped-Δt-division baseline suffices without running it, and I will not invent that result. It is a revision target, not a floor.
- No anti-pattern composition with an uninsertable mitigation.
- D does not collapse: a falsification is constructible — NC-A on a genuinely irregular cohort with a corrected predicted landing point is a real test.

**Soft judgment: `revise`.** The idea is well-positioned and the mechanism fits the gap it names; what is not yet believable is the instrument. Six of the attacks above are `major` and `answerable now`, meaning the design can be repaired on paper before any GPU is touched — but as currently specified, a favourable result would not isolate the claimed cause from encoder capacity, censoring handling, informative observation intensity, or the λ parameterisation, and one of the four benchmarks is asked to demonstrate a property it does not have.

**revision_targets[]**

1. **Add the missing intermediate baseline and the operator-only ablation.** Run DeepTCSR with Δt as an encoder input and its renormalisation evaluated at S(Δt_j) with the denominator clamped — the naive version anyone builds first — and a SurvTD arm with the duration discount kept but the division restored. Without these two arms the paper's second contribution (the operator substitution) is defended only by mass conservation and non-expansiveness and is never measured.
2. **Declare the sampling protocol per benchmark, and publish the cohort property table.** Per cohort: censoring rate, informative-censoring plausibility, and the distribution of Δt (dispersion, not just mean). Either state the irregularisation protocol applied to C-MAPSS or move it out of the irregularity claim — natively it is uniform-cycle sampled with a labelled, uncensored test split, which makes it a placebo cohort by the candidate's own definition, not the site of the headline drop.
3. **Repair NC-A.** Write γ_j into the Step 5 target so there is something to intervene on; split the ablation into discount-only and shift-only arms; restate the predicted landing point as what the discount-only arm can actually produce rather than "the unit-step consistency baseline"; and either demonstrate Δt = δ_s on the regular control cohort or replace the no-op-by-construction claim with a measured null.
4. **Fix the two orthogonal controls.** Replace NC-C with a count-geometric λ at matched effective horizon (λ → 1.0 removes bootstrapping and does not test Step 7's claim), and make NC-B a within-subject interval permutation preserving total follow-up so it breaks alignment without corrupting τ_j − t_j — noting in the write-up that even then it does not separate temporal alignment from observation-intensity leakage, which needs its own arm.
5. **Add the irregularity dose-response sweep on the simulated cohorts, and a shared-encoder protocol.** A monotone gain-vs-Δt-dispersion curve that vanishes at zero dispersion is the strongest available evidence for the claimed cause; one backbone with four losses swapped is what makes any main-table margin attributable to the loss rather than the encoder.
6. **Fix the metric set.** Define the alarm-stability metric that the Lee2019 delta already promises; add a proper calibration measure to support the "calibration degrades" prediction; report risk-stratified and near-horizon results so the tail-concentrated failure mode the motivation describes is actually observable; and add a direct diagnostic of that failure mode (target-distribution entropy or renormalisation magnitude in the high-risk stratum).
7. **Re-source or strike the 0.03 bar and state the statistical protocol.** Name the paper, cohort and measured seed SD, or drop the number. Declare one primary benchmark and one primary metric, state the test, and model test-set sampling variance (bootstrap CIs or repeated splits) rather than model-seed variance alone — on PBC the latter is the smaller of the two.
8. **Rebudget or rescope.** Eighteen GPU-hours does not cover ~340 trainings including a MIMIC-IV sequence model and four C-MAPSS sub-datasets; a hyperparameter search "shared" across four heterogeneous cohorts is not parity. Name the four baseline methods and state which implementations are the authors' own.
