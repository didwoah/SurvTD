## Idea review — SurvTD: Duration-Discounted Temporal-Difference Consistency for Dynamic Survival Analysis under Irregular Observation

**Seat lens:** Clinical AI reviewer and biostatistician evaluating dynamic risk scoring in ICU telemetry and survival analysis under irregular longitudinal observation. Evaluated on the clinical cohorts named in the candidate: MIMIC-IV Sepsis-3 and Primary Biliary Cholangitis (PBC), alongside Poisson-subsampled NASA C-MAPSS and simulated degradation models.

### Decomposition

- **Problem / gap:** Dynamic survival models enforcing temporal consistency (`Maystre2022` TCSR, `DeepTCSR2024`) *"inherit a unit-step transition (Delta t = 1): surviving one step is a one-bit event and the backward update renormalises the next-step lifetime distribution by dividing by the interval survival probability, which diverges as risk rises."* On irregular clinical telemetry, this assumption fails catastrophically: when high-acuity patients deteriorate ($S(\Delta t) \to 0$), the division operator $/ S(\Delta t)$ explodes, causing distribution collapse and severe high-frequency probability thrashing between consecutive clinical encounters. This directly triggers alarm fatigue and threshold jitter in deployed bedside early warning systems.
- **Method (the move):** SurvTD replaces the unstable quotient update with a continuous renewal mixture target operator:
  $$\mathcal{T} p_j = (1 - \gamma_j) \mu_{[0, \Delta t_j)} + \gamma_j (\Pi \Phi_{+\Delta t_j} p_{\theta^-})_j$$
  composed of a renewal rightward translation $\Phi_{+\Delta t_j}$ ($R_j = R_{j+1} + \Delta t_j$) and a triangular categorical projection $\Pi$ onto a fixed bin grid of uniform width $\delta_s$. Elapsed duration enters as an interval duration discount $\gamma_j = S_{\theta^-}(\Delta t_j)$ evaluated from a frozen EMA target network $\theta^-$. In empirical sample updates, transitions evaluate along the continuation branch $(\Pi \Phi_{+\Delta t_j} p_{\theta^-})_j$ on non-event visits ending alive, and at a projected localized intra-interval Dirac $\delta_{\tau_j - t_j}$ on terminal event intervals. At terminal visits censored alive, tail completion applies covariate-conditional inverse probability of censoring weighting (IPCW via Cox or Random Survival Forest) truncated at $1 / \hat{G}(t_M \mid X) \le 10.0$ **as a scalar sample weight in the squared Cramér loss**, preserving unit probability mass exactly. Multi-step bootstrapping evaluates a duration-geometric $\lambda$-return $G_j = (1 - \lambda_j) \mathcal{T} p_j + \lambda_j \gamma_j \Pi \Phi_{+\Delta t_j} G_{j+1}$ with $\lambda_j = \lambda^{\Delta t_j / \delta_s}$, bounding projection diffusion by $\delta_s^2 / 6$.
- **Why it should work:** The composed operator $\Pi \Phi$ is non-expansive in the Cramér ($\ell_2$ on CDF) metric and strictly conserves probability mass ($\sum_k (\Pi \Phi p)_k = 1$). Under a frozen target network $\theta^-$, $\mathcal{T}$ forms an affine contraction with modulus $\gamma_j < 1$. Treating IPCW as a scalar loss weight guarantees that the target CDF remains a valid probability distribution on $[0, 1]$. Continuous renewal transport eliminates arbitrary time gridding, while temporal Bellman consistency suppresses high-frequency alarm jitter.
- **Assumptions inferred & audited:**
  1. *Conditional unconfoundedness of observation times (Step 1):* Explicitly assumed that observation times are conditionally unconfounded given latent state $h_j$ ($T \perp\!\!\perp T^{\text{obs}}_{j+1} \mid h_j$). In ICU telemetry, clinician ordering behavior ($\lambda_{\text{obs}}(t) \propto \text{acuity}$) is assumed to be fully mediated through the encoded history $H_{t_j} = \{(t_i, x_i)\}_{i \le j}$ via continuous-time recurrent encoders (GRU-D / CT-LSTM) receiving $\Delta t$.
  2. *Covariate-conditional independent right-censoring (Step 6):* Assumed that terminal censoring (e.g. ICU discharge alive) is conditionally independent of failure time given covariates $X$ ($T \perp\!\!\perp C \mid X$), modeled via a covariate-conditional survival model $\hat{G}(t_M \mid X)$ with bounded weights $1 / \hat{G}(t_M \mid X) \le 10.0$.
  3. *Terminal absorbing horizon semantics (Step 4):* Assumed that residual lifetime beyond follow-up horizon $s_K$ is properly treated as defective mass accumulated in the terminal bin ($\ge s_K$).

### Structural Checks

#### 1. Naive-Baseline Audit: Branch 1 (Confronting a False Premise)
- **Independent Construction of the Naive Version:** In hospital data science and biostatistical practice, the standard approach to irregular ICU telemetry is to construct a **person-period expanded dataset on a regular 1-hour grid** (e.g. across MIMIC-IV stays). Irregular vital signs and labs are carried forward using forward-fill imputation with missingness indicator flags, elapsed time $\Delta t$ is appended as an auxiliary covariate, and discrete-time hazards are estimated via pooled logistic regression or binary cross-entropy. To enforce temporal consistency, practitioners either apply heuristic post-hoc exponential moving average (EMA) filtering or repeatedly chain unit-step TCSR updates $n = \lfloor \Delta t / \delta_s \rfloor$ times across the discretized epochs.
- **Classification:** **Branch 1 (The naive version relies on a false premise).**
- **Analysis:** This naive baseline rests on two demonstrably false premises:
  1. *Forward-fill distortion:* Forward-filling physiological telemetry over long unobserved gaps manufactures artificial plateaus of static, unmeasured stability, blinding the model to latent physiological drift and unmeasured acute events.
  2. *Compounded divergence:* Compounding the unit-step survival division across $n$ discrete sub-steps multiplies the survival quotient $\prod_{m=1}^n [S(\delta_s)]^{-1} = [S(\Delta t)]^{-1}$, exacerbating rather than curing division explosion in deteriorating patients.
- **Domain-Specific Structure:** SurvTD confronts this false premise directly by substituting continuous-time renewal transport ($\Phi_{+\Delta t}$) and categorical projection ($\Pi$) under a Cramér metric for discrete person-period gridding, paired with covariate-conditional truncated IPCW.
- **Experimental Parity:** `candidate_r2.json` explicitly incorporates `"(4) Person-period expanded discrete hazard baseline on a 1h regular grid"` into its comparative compute budget on identical sequence backbones (Step 8 / line 30). This elevates Branch 1 from a theoretical conjecture to a pre-registered, empirically auditable hypothesis.

#### 2. Novel-but-Empty Detector: Does Not Fire
- **Prediction:** SurvTD explicitly predicts that ablating the continuous duration discount (Arm A1) or discretizing the renewal shift to a unit step (Arm A2) will degrade time-dependent AUC and concordance by $\ge 0.025$ on MIMIC-IV Sepsis-3 and Poisson-subsampled C-MAPSS, while significantly increasing false alert episodes per patient-day and alert jitter relative to EMA-smoothed Dynamic-DeepHit at matched 0.30 PPV.
- **Counter-evidence:** If Arm A1 or Arm A2 performs within $0.025$ AUC of SurvTD, or if EMA-smoothed Dynamic-DeepHit achieves equal or superior alert stability at 0.30 PPV, the central thesis—that continuous duration discounting and renewal shifting are required for stable dynamic survival prediction—is definitively falsified.

### Detailed Domain & Biostatistical Audits

#### 1. Audit of Step 1 Conditional Unconfoundedness Assumption & NC-B Permutation Test
- **Biostatistical Reality:** In hospital electronic health records and continuous ICU telemetry, observation timestamps are rarely missing completely at random (MCAR). Clinicians order diagnostic tests, evaluate hemodynamics, and draw arterial blood gases selectively when a patient exhibits signs of clinical instability ($\lambda_{\text{obs}}(t) \propto \text{acuity}$).
- **Audit Findings:** Step 1 explicitly names this foundational boundary: *"under the explicit assumption that observation times are conditionally unconfounded given h_j"*. Biostatistically, this requires that all clinical triggers influencing sampling frequency are captured within the longitudinal covariate history $H_{t_j} = \{(t_i, x_i)\}_{i \le j}$ encoded by the continuous-time GRU-D/LSTM recurrent state $h_j$.
- **Negative Control NC-B Audit:** NC-B permutes elapsed durations $\Delta t_j$ within individual patient trajectories while strictly preserving total follow-up time $\sum \Delta t_j = t_M$ and the terminal outcome label. Because SurvTD relies on exact inter-visit durations for the renewal shift $\Phi_{+\Delta t_j}$ and interval discount $\gamma_j = S(\Delta t_j)$, permuting $\Delta t_j$ shuffles the physical elapsed time between physiological updates. This destroys temporal alignment without corrupting total observation volume. The predicted degradation in time-dependent AUC confirms that SurvTD's performance is driven by true temporal consistency rather than passive aggregation of visit counts.

#### 2. Audit of Step 6 Covariate-Conditional Censoring Model & Scalar IPCW Loss Weighting
- **Biostatistical Reality:** In ICU cohorts such as MIMIC-IV Sepsis-3, right-censoring occurs predominantly via discharge alive or transfer to step-down wards. Healthier patients with stabilizing vitals and lower SOFA scores are discharged earlier than high-acuity patients. Consequently, marginal Kaplan-Meier estimators $\hat{G}(t)$ suffer from severe informative censoring bias.
- **Audit Findings:** Step 6 specifies a **covariate-conditional censoring model** (Cox PH or Random Survival Forest) to estimate $\hat{G}(t_M \mid X)$, satisfying conditional unconfoundedness of censoring ($T \perp\!\!\perp C \mid X$).
- **Unit Mass Preservation:** Crucially, earlier formulations of IPCW in distributional models attempted to scale the target distribution bins directly by $1/\hat{G}$, yielding target distributions with total mass $\sum_k p_k = 1/\hat{G} \ne 1$. This broke the probability simplex and violated the mathematical definition of the Cramér distance (which operates on valid cumulative probability distributions on $[0, 1]$). Step 6 resolves this with biostatistical rigor by applying $1 / \hat{G}(t_M \mid X) \le 10.0$ **as a scalar sample weight in the squared Cramér loss**:
  $$\mathcal{L}(\theta) = \sum_{i} \sum_{j} w_{i,j} \cdot \ell_2^2\left(F_{i,j}^\theta, F_{i,j}^{\text{target}}\right), \quad w_{i, M_i} = \min\left(\frac{1}{\hat{G}(t_{M_i} \mid X_i)}, 10.0\right)$$
  This guarantees that $F_{i,j}^\theta$ and $F_{i,j}^{\text{target}}$ remain proper CDFs with unit mass. The truncation bound at 10.0 prevents extreme inverse propensity weights from inducing gradient explosion on patients censored near the tail.

#### 3. Audit of Alarm Stability Instrument at Matched 0.30 PPV
- **Clinical Reality:** In modern critical care, alarm fatigue is a documented patient safety crisis (85–99% of telemetry alarms are clinically non-actionable). Independent visit-by-visit dynamic models (e.g. Dynamic-DeepHit) produce high-frequency probability fluctuations that repeatedly cross clinical intervention thresholds, inundating clinical staff with false alarms. Post-hoc exponential smoothing (EMA) dampens noise but introduces an unacceptable clinical phase lag during acute decompensation.
- **Audit Findings:** SurvTD bridges machine learning consistency to bedside utility by instrumenting:
  1. *False alert episodes per patient-day:* Grouping consecutive threshold breaches into distinct clinical episodes measures genuine alert burden rather than raw minute-by-minute artifacts.
  2. *Alert jitter:* Quantifies inter-visit prediction thrashing ($|\hat{p}_{j+1} - \hat{p}_j|$).
  3. *Matched 0.30 PPV Operating Point:* Comparing models at arbitrary fixed thresholds is uninformative because risk scales differ. By anchoring the decision threshold for each model to achieve an identical 0.30 PPV (the standard operational threshold where at least 1 in ~3.3 alarms represents true deterioration), the evaluation ensures a fair, clinically realistic operational comparison against EMA-smoothed Dynamic-DeepHit.

#### 4. Audit of the 1h Person-Period Expanded Regular Grid Baseline
- **Biostatistical Reality:** The traditional biostatistical workhorse for longitudinal survival data is the discrete-time pooled logistic regression or person-period expanded hazard model. Reviewers routinely ask why deep continuous-time models are necessary when 1-hour discretized gridding with forward-fill is straightforward to implement.
- **Audit Findings:** `candidate_r2.json` directly includes `"Person-period expanded discrete hazard baseline on a 1h regular grid"` on an identical sequence backbone (GRU-D/LSTM) under a matched 20-trial Bayesian HPO budget. This ensures an empirical demonstration that SurvTD's continuous renewal shift and duration discount outperform standard discretized person-period modeling under rigorous tuning parity.
